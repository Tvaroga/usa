# USA Road Trip — San Francisco → Los Angeles → Las Vegas

**Car:** Chevrolet Equinox (or similar) — Thrifty
**Pickup:** San Francisco Airport (SFO), 16 Aug 2026, 15:00
**Return:** Las Vegas (McCarran) Airport (LAS), 28 Aug 2026, 20:00
**Duration:** 12 nights / 13 days

---

## The route at a glance

```
SF (2 nights) → Yosemite (3) → Sequoia (1)
→ Los Angeles (3) → Las Vegas (3, incl. parks day trips) → fly home
```

Total driving is roughly **1,900–2,100 km (1,200–1,300 mi)**. It's a drive-heavy trip, but every leg has scenery. Because we go **inland through the Sierra parks**, we trade away the Big Sur coast[...]

## Day by day

### Day 1 — Sun 16 Aug · Arrive + San Francisco
- Pick up car at SFO at 15:00. Drive into the city (~30 min).
- Evening: Fisherman's Wharf, Pier 39 (sea lions), dinner on the waterfront.
- **Overnight:** San Francisco.

### Day 2 — Mon 17 Aug · San Francisco (your "one day")
- **Morning: Alcatraz Island** — ferry to the famous former prison (book well ahead — tickets sell out).
- **Cable car** ride over the hills + **Lombard Street** (the crooked street).
- **Lunch:** Ferry Building Marketplace or Fisherman's Wharf / Pier 39 (sea lions).
- **Afternoon (pick one):**
  - Walk out onto the **Golden Gate Bridge**, or drive to **Battery Spencer / Marin Headlands** for the classic view.
  - **Golden Gate Park** — California Academy of Sciences, de Young Museum, Japanese Tea Garden.
  - **Ferry to Sausalito** and back — the scenic bay crossing without the bikes.
- **Late afternoon:** Painted Ladies at Alamo Square, Chinatown / North Beach, or Twin Peaks viewpoint at sunset.
- **Overnight:** San Francisco.

### Day 3 — Tue 18 Aug · SF → Yosemite (~4 hrs, 300 km)
- Drive east into the Sierra Nevada, entering Yosemite from the west (Hwy 120 Big Oak Flat or Hwy 140).
- Afternoon: first look at **Yosemite Valley** — Tunnel View, Bridalveil Fall, valley meadows.
- **Overnight:** Yosemite Valley (book very early) or Oakhurst / Mariposa — same base for the next 3 nights.

### Day 4 — Wed 19 Aug · Yosemite Valley
- **Tunnel View**, **Glacier Point** (huge valley panorama), Yosemite Valley loop.
- El Capitan, Half Dome, Bridalveil Fall; easy valley-floor walks or the **Mist Trail**.
- **Overnight:** same.

### Day 5 — Thu 20 Aug · Yosemite high country (the day gained from Tahoe)
- Drive **Tioga Road** east: **Tenaya Lake, Tuolumne Meadows, Olmsted Point** — alpine lakes and granite domes (swim at Tenaya Lake). This gets you the Tioga scenery you'd have seen from the Tahoe s[...]
- Alternatively: a bigger hike, the **Mariposa Grove** of giant sequoias, or a relaxed morning.
- **Overnight:** same.

### Day 6 — Fri 21 Aug · Yosemite → Sequoia & Kings Canyon (~4 hrs, 220 km)
- **Giant sequoias** — the largest trees on Earth. See the **General Sherman Tree**, Giant Forest, and climb **Moro Rock** for the view.
- **Overnight:** Three Rivers or Visalia.

### Day 7 — Sat 22 Aug · Sequoia → Los Angeles (~3.5–4 hrs, 330 km)
- Drive south to LA. Arrive afternoon.
- Ease in with sunset at **Santa Monica Pier** or Venice Beach.
- **Overnight:** Los Angeles (Santa Monica or Hollywood area is convenient).

### Day 8 — Sun 23 Aug · Universal Studios Hollywood
- Full day at **Universal Studios** (buy tickets online in advance; consider Express pass in summer). The Studio Tour, Super Nintendo World, Harry Potter world.
- **Overnight:** Los Angeles.

### Day 9 — Mon 24 Aug · LA beaches + city
- Beaches: **Santa Monica, Hermosa, Malibu**; Venice boardwalk.
- Plus Hollywood Walk of Fame, Griffith Observatory (great city views), or Beverly Hills / Rodeo Drive.
- **Overnight:** Los Angeles.

### Day 10 — Tue 25 Aug · LA → Las Vegas via Hoover Dam (~4.5–5 hrs, 440 km)
- Drive through the Mojave Desert.
- Detour to **Hoover Dam** (about 45 min from Vegas).
- Evening: the **Las Vegas Strip** — Bellagio fountains, Fremont Street.
- **Overnight:** Las Vegas.

### Day 11 — Wed 26 Aug · Zion National Park day trip (~2.5–3 hrs each way)
- **Zion** — red gorges, the Virgin River, shuttle up the canyon. Easy Riverside Walk or the Emerald Pools.
- *Option:* stay overnight near Zion/Springdale to also see **Bryce Canyon** the next morning (see Trade-offs).
- **Overnight:** Las Vegas (or Zion if extending).

### Day 12 — Thu 27 Aug · Grand Canyon (West) or free day + **Cirque du Soleil "O"**
- **Grand Canyon West / Skywalk** (~2.5 hrs) is the doable day trip from Vegas. (The famous South Rim is ~4.5 hrs one-way — better as part of a longer eastern loop.)
- Or keep it relaxed: pool day + shopping.
- **Evening: Cirque du Soleil "O" at the Bellagio** — a water show. **Book this in advance** and schedule it for tonight (not the 28th) so it doesn't clash with your car return.
- **Overnight:** Las Vegas.

### Day 13 — Fri 28 Aug · Vegas shopping + departure
- **Shopping:** Las Vegas North & South Premium Outlets, Forum Shops at Caesars, Grand Canal Shoppes.
- Late lunch, then **return car at LAS by 20:00**.

## Book these ahead (summer sells out)
- **Universal Studios** tickets (+ Express pass if budget allows)
- **Cirque du Soleil "O"** — pick a night that isn't your departure day
- **Alcatraz** (if interested) — often sold out weeks ahead
- **Yosemite lodging** — and check whether a **day-use/peak-hours reservation** is required for 2026
- **Zion** — if interested in hiking Angels Landing, it needs a **permit lottery** (the Narrows/Riverside Walk do not)
- Hotels in Yosemite/Sequoia gateway towns fill fast in August

## Driving & practical tips
- Get an **unlimited-mileage** rate and confirm the one-way SFO→LAS drop-off is included.
- Buy the **America the Beautiful annual parks pass** ($250 at recreation.gov) — **one pass per vehicle covers all passengers**. Covers Yosemite, Sequoia, Zion, Grand Canyon and more. Available to international visitors; purchase online before arrival at https://www.recreation.gov/pass/
- August is **very hot** in the desert (Vegas, Zion, Grand Canyon often 38–43 °C). Start hikes early, carry lots of water.
- Download **offline maps** — cell coverage is patchy in the parks and desert.
- Note the US **rental-car minimum age is usually 21** (often 25).
